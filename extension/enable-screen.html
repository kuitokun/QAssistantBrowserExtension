QAssistant

Turn on screen capture

This optional permission lets QAssistant show your browser bar, the taskbar and the Windows clock in screenshots.

  • You choose the screen in your browser's own window.
  • Your browser shows a sharing bar while it's on.
  • One image is taken only when you click capture.
  • Sharing stops after 10 minutes without a capture.
  • Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
